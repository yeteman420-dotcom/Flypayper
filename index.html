The Story

Deep in the forgotten woodlands of the Pacific Northwest, a team of documentary filmmakers ventures into territory marked on no map. Locals whisper of Squach — a presence older than the trees, drawn to those who dare look too long into the dark.

When their guide vanishes without a footprint, the crew's cameras capture something that should not exist. Something that watches back. As night falls and the forest closes in, they realize the legend was never a myth. It was a warning.

Some things are better left unseen.
