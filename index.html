The System

Three Moves. Infinite Possibilities.

Our patent-pending Thread-Lock system turns a simple twist into a secure, customizable connection. No tools. No hassle. Just you and your style.

🔓
Step 01

Align & Drop

Position your MODTOE module over the threaded post built into the sole. The tapered guide ensures perfect alignment every time.

↻
Step 02

Twist to Lock

Rotate the module 360 degrees clockwise. The precision-machined threads engage with a satisfying click — torque-rated to 15 Nm.

✓
Step 03

Go Anywhere

Walk, run, climb. The locking mechanism self-tightens under load. When you are ready to switch, a simple counter-twist releases it.
